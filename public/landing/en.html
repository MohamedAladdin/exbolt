09 / FAQ

What teams ask
before they start.

If your question isn't here, send it over — we answer the same day.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?
Not necessarily. You can connect an existing number as long as it isn't active on the WhatsApp Business app. If it is, we set up a dedicated number for you.
Where is my data stored?
On a dedicated Supabase deployment with per-account isolation and RLS. Scale customers can request a separate deployment on their own server.
Does it support multiple branches?
Yes. Scale includes branch management and per-branch permissions, so each branch sees only its own conversations and deals.
Is there an API?
Scale includes API keys and webhooks. Growth includes ready-made integrations and flows.
How long does onboarding take?
48 to 72 hours after Meta Business and the number are ready. We go through the steps with you.
Are WhatsApp fees included?
No. Meta bills message fees directly to your account. We help you understand them before you start.
